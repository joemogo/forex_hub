## Ranking principle

From the Phase 1 checklist: **seven of the ten blocking rows — stop placement, take-profit
methodology, post-entry management, exit, break-even, partials and scaling — are all most likely to be
answered by the same single artifact: one complete, narrated trade from setup to close.**

A topical video about stop-losses answers one row. A walkthrough answers seven. The queue is ordered
accordingly, which is why **target profiles outrank topical searches** even though a profile has no URL yet.
