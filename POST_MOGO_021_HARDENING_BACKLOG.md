| B-25 | **Forward RECOVERABILITY is capped at 25 trades, and the cap tightens with every close.** This is split out of B-22 because B-22 states "historical only; no forward capture gap", and while the *capture* claim remains correct — no live close has ever been missed — the *recoverability* claim is not. `evidenceCaptureClosedTrades` snapshots `alexGAccount.closedPositions.slice(0,25)` (`index.html:15951`), and `closedPositions` is newest-first.
